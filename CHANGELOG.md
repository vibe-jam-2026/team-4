# Changelog

Every published iteration of the game gets a version number. The current version is shown at the bottom of the menu, the pause screen and the game-over screen.

## v13 (2026-11-10)
- Armory: a shop in the menu where rings buy five extra missiles (Wasp, Lance, Striker, Longbow, Sunfire) with 2× to 9× boss damage, different round counts, reload times and blast radii. The equipped one is carried on top of the standard missiles and fires with V.
- Fixed the bottom-left HUD overlap: the Flares button, the armory-missile button and the microphone badge now share one row (stacked on phones).

## v12 (2026-11-10)
- The in-game HUD shows "Esc pause" next to the pause button (Esc still ejects).
- Lane changes work right up to a corner: near a corner, left/right queues the turn and still moves you a lane. Before, the turn swallowed the lane change, and at a T-junction you could not change lane at all.
- Shout for flares fires reliably: much lower trigger levels for laptop microphones, a live microphone test in the menu ("Heard you! Flares would fire ✓"), and the microphone turns back on at take-off if it was on last time.
- Version numbers and this changelog.

## v11 (2026-11-10)
- Pilot debrief after every run: grade, reaction times, threats handled, weapon use and coaching notes.
- Landing and taxi to the hangar after each won level.

## v10 (2026-11-10)
- Flares trigger on any loud noise into the laptop microphone, with a sensitivity slider.

## v9 (2026-11-10)
- Flight path map on the end screens.
- Slow motion when boss fire is incoming, to give time to call flares.

## v8 (2026-11-10)
- Flares that decoy boss fire, with a voice command.
- New trailer reel using the game's own audio.

## v7 (2026-11-09)
- Original music: synthwave cruise track and a rock-synth boss anthem.
- Wingmen block boss fire.

## v6 (2026-11-09)
- Final level 4 "Storm" with the Sea Monster boss.
- Bosses climb over walls, glowing night mines, easier level 3 boss, first trailer reel.

## v5 (2026-11-09)
- Campaign mode: three levels with bosses and their own maps.
- Loyal wingmen and Space to restart.

## v4 (2026-11-09)
- Fighter-style turns, barrel roll, loop, enemy fighters, hangar skins, local leaderboard, open-air track.

## v3 (2026-11-09)
- Sky theme, missiles, clearer turns, new menu fonts and the Esc ejection fakeout.

## v2 (2026-11-09)
- Retheme to a space-lane flyer with a JAS 39 Gripen E, renamed Wings of War Forever.

## v1 (2026-11-09)
- First playable version: an endless 3D runner in the browser.
